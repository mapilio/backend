# Community Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Mapilio welcomes respectful collaboration across mapping, imagery, geospatial, backend, mobile, AI, operations, and community disciplines. Participants are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and technical honesty;
- welcome different experience levels and backgrounds;
- give actionable feedback about work rather than attacking people;
- respect privacy, consent, attribution, and open mapping community norms;
- disclose conflicts of interest and correct mistakes openly;
- stop behavior when another participant or a maintainer says it is harmful.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate humiliation, doxxing, stalking, impersonation, spam, and publishing another person's private or sensitive data are unacceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project events, and other spaces where someone represents the Mapilio project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, avoid retaliation, and protect the privacy of reporters and affected people.

Do not publish conduct reports in an issue or pull request. Before accepting community contributions, the owners must designate and test a confidential conduct-reporting channel and identify at least two maintainers responsible for handling reports. Until that gate is complete, contribution intake and community promotion remain blocked.

Good-faith disagreement with a technical or governance decision is not misconduct. Repeated disruptive behavior, privacy violations, or retaliation after a boundary is set may be.
